AI MEMORY UPDATE #2 — paste into Settings → AI Memory → Information,
appended to whatever's there (including the previous update). Covers
what's changed since that last pass.

CLASS SCALING (new since last update):
- Player Card abilities and base class stats now scale by RANK (E through SS), not level. Each class grows differently by design — Paladin/Druid/Knight are tanky (high HP/Defense, low Speed), Assassin is pure speed (exceeds even Champion-level Speed by SS), Mage/Necromancer stay glass-cannon even at the top (Power beyond Champion baseline, HP/Defense below it). All 10 converge to be roughly power-comparable with a Kingdom Champion by SS-rank without being identical to each other.

LOSING HAS REAL CONSEQUENCES (new since last update):
- Losing to a political hierarchy NPC (Warriors through Champion, not creatures, not Primordials) now costs real XP, scaled to who beat you. Can cascade into losing a level, and a big enough drop can cost a rank. Shown clearly on the defeat screen. Kingdoms already unlocked are NOT re-locked if rank drops — only future rank-ups (unlocking new kingdoms) are affected.
- Surrendering any fight costs gold instead (unchanged mechanic, now consistently shown on the loss screen too).

CHAMPION MYSTERY REWARD (new since last update):
- Defeating a Champion now triggers a choice of 3 face-down cards. 2 hold gold (1500 or 2500), 1 holds a genuine random ability belonging to that Champion — granted as a real card if picked. Freshly randomized every single time, for every Champion, no pattern.

WIN/LOSS PRESENTATION (new since last update):
- Both "You Won" and "You Lost" now show as a large banner with real details underneath (XP gained/lost, ability unlocked out of how many the creature has, level/rank changes) and stay on screen until the player clicks "Next" to return to the World Map — no auto-redirect.

Anything in prior AI Memory notes describing flat/unscaled Player Card abilities, or losing having no consequence, is now OUTDATED.
