COMBAT SYSTEM (current, as of this update):
- Every fight (creature, hierarchy, Champion, Primordial, party, PvP) runs on one shared engine: classes, cards, energy, the Elemental Wheel, and full ability effects.
- Turn order is Speed-based — the faster side acts first every exchange, not fixed alternation. A fast enemy can act before the player.
- A "card" is a single ability. Every player's Player Card (their class's 3 abilities) is auto-included in every fight; creature cards (0-3) are optional on top of it.
- Player Card abilities and base class stats scale by RANK (E through SS), not level. Each class has a distinct growth identity — some are tanky, some glass-cannon, some pure speed — converging to be power-comparable with Kingdom Champions by SS-rank, not identical to each other.
- Ability effects are fully real, not flavor text: damage, buffs/debuffs, shields, dodge, reflect, revive, defense-piercing, HP/energy steal, forced skip-turn, ability-copy, conditional bonus damage.
- Losing to a political hierarchy NPC (Warriors through Champion) costs real XP, scaled to who beat you — can cascade into losing a level or a rank. Creature and Primordial losses carry no such penalty.
- Gear (weapon/armor/consumable) is chosen per-fight from the Pre-Battle screen and gives real stat effects, pulled from the actual Shop catalog — separate from permanent Inventory equip state.

PROGRESSION:
- Rank comes from XP (any fight, or League KDA sync), and the XP curve genuinely steepens at higher levels.
- Reaching D/C/B/A-rank unlocks new kingdoms at random (1/2/2/3, all 9 by A-rank).
- Creature rank access is gated by total kingdoms unlocked: B/A need 3+, S needs 5+, SS needs 7+.

ENDGAME — PvG:
- Primordial Gods (one per kingdom) are the true endgame. Unlocked when all 9 kingdoms are conquered, all 9 Champions defeated, and every SS-rank creature across every kingdom defeated at least once.
- Primordial fights require a party at first; solo access to the remaining ones opens after a player has been part of a party that defeated 3 different Primordials.
- Defeating a Primordial grants both its unique ability cards directly, a large gold reward, and triple SS-rank XP.

Anything about "hidden passives," "flat weapon/armor bonuses," or "flavor-only abilities" is OUTDATED — none of that is true of the current system anymore.
