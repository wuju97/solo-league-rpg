Welcome, Adventurer

Solonia is a world of nine kingdoms, each with its own people, politics, and creatures — and you're here to make a name for yourself in it.

Fight creatures and political hierarchies to earn XP and gold, rank up from E all the way to SS, and unlock new kingdoms as you go. Duel other players, form parties, take on Kingdom Champions, and — once you've conquered enough of the world — challenge the Primordial Gods themselves.

Start with the World Map to explore and fight, check Player to build your character, and visit Gameplay in the left sidebar any time for the full rules.
