# GUILD RULES

## Organization Growth
Guilds change visibly over time through world events and community achievements —
e.g. the Adventurer Guild starting as one building, later expanding into training
grounds, shops, tournaments. Not a fixed, static entity.

## World Elections
Some organizations elect leaders. Players can influence outcomes via reputation,
quests, donations, endorsements, or uncovering scandals. A new leader can change
prices, quest availability, political alliances, research priorities.

## Hidden Membership
Some ordinary-seeming NPCs secretly belong to major organizations (a kind innkeeper
who's actually a spy, a fisherman in the Order of Nine, a bard who couriers for the
Keepers of History). Discoverable only after extended interaction — should never be
told to the player upfront.

## Guild Rank
Recruit → Member → Veteran → Officer → Master → Grandmaster. Different guilds unlock
different content per rank.
