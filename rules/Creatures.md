# CREATURE RULES

## Philosophy
Not every creature is a monster — spectrum: Animals → Wildlife → Magical Beasts →
Monsters → Elite Monsters → Legendary Creatures → World Bosses → Ancient Titans →
Celestial Beings → Primordial Entities.

## Threat Rank
F, E, D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS, Legendary, Mythic, Titan, Primordial — indicates danger,
not just power level.

## Creature Intelligence
Mindless → Instinctive → Animal → Pack Intelligence → Human Intelligence → Ancient
Intelligence → Unknown Intelligence. Some negotiate, some remember players, some never
attack first.

## Behavior, Territory & Evolution
Real AI per creature (hunt/sleep/patrol/den/raise young/migrate/avoid fire/flee
Titans) rather than random spawning. Creatures hold home/migration/breeding/
hunting/resting/seasonal territories players can learn. Some evolve based on age,
environment, magic, and world events (e.g. Wolf → Dire Wolf → Alpha → Ancient →
Spirit Wolf).

## Weather Behavior & Ecosystem
Weather affects creature behavior (rain strengthens some monsters, snow moves ice
creatures, fog brings out shadow monsters, meteor showers draw celestial creatures).
Full predator/prey chain reactions: removing wolves increases foxes, decreases
rabbits, grows plants back, migrates herbivores, and shifts NPC quests in response.

## Taming & Companions
Some creatures become genuine companions (not simple pets) — trust grows, they hunt
alongside the player, learn commands, protect, can breed, age, and die.

## Creature Memory & Families
Intelligent creatures remember players (help a griffin, it recognizes you months
later; kill its mate, its offspring hunt you down years later). Creatures reproduce,
nest, raise young, migrate, and die — full life cycles players can witness.

## Creature Research
Every creature should have an encyclopedia page: habitat, diet, drops, weakness,
behavior, lore, rare variants, breeding season, known locations, research %. Filled by
observing, tracking, fighting, or studying — not just killing.

## Rare Variants
Most species have uncommon forms: Albino, Melanistic, Crystal-touched, Emberborn,
Stormcharged, Ancient, Spirit-bound, Corrupted, Celestial — different behavior,
appearance, and rewards.
