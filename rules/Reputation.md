# REPUTATION RULES

## Tracking
Every organization (kingdom, guild, faction) independently tracks: Friendship,
Respect, Influence, Trust, Favor, Rank, Debt. Being a hero to one faction doesn't
automatically impress another — no shared/global reputation number.

## Kingdom Reputation Tiers
Citizen → Trusted → Honored → Hero → Champion → Legend. Each kingdom progresses
independently. Ties into Shop pricing (already implemented as a discount table:
Citizen normal, Trusted 5%, Hero 10%, Champion 15%, Legend 20%).

## Faction Wars
Organizations argue and compete (Merchant Guild vs Explorer Guild over funding,
Hunters vs Researchers over conservation, Engineers vs Nature Wardens over
deforestation, Temple Guardians vs Archaeologists over tombs). These generate quests
with morally mixed outcomes rather than clean good/bad choices.

## Building Reputation
Reputation with a kingdom should build from: quest completion count in that kingdom,
number of times helping the same NPC repeatedly (this also builds individual NPC
trust, separate from kingdom reputation — see NPC.md), donations, combat victories on
their behalf, and story-relevant choices.
