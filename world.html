Hit List

Each kingdom's political ladder — Warriors, Captains, Nobles, Generals, King, and its Champion. Defeat everyone in a tier to unlock the next. Defeated NPCs are on a 2-day cooldown before you can fight them again (3 days for a Champion) — and defeating a Champion resets this whole ladder, requiring a full re-climb from Warriors to challenge them a second time.
