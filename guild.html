Guilds

Philosophy

Every NPC belongs to something. Every city belongs to something. Every kingdom has politics, every guild has rivals, every organization has secrets. There are four major categories: Kingdom Organizations, Universal Guilds, Secret Orders, and Criminal Syndicates.

Kingdom Organizations

Every kingdom has organizations unique to it, beyond what's covered in each kingdom's Politics tab.

👑 Valoria

Royal Valorian Legion (largest military in Solonia — Knights, Archers, Cavalry, Scouts, Battle Mages, Engineers, elite Silver Guard), the Hall of Justice (law, trials, judges, investigations), the Merchant Assembly (taxes, trade routes, markets), and the Royal Intelligence Bureau (official spies, unknown to most citizens).

❄ Frosthaven

The White Wolves (elite monster hunters), the Frostguard (protect the royal family), the Glacier Rangers (explore frozen lands), and the Brotherhood of Hunters (largest hunting organization).

🌳 Eldergrove

The Circle of Roots (protects the World Tree), the Forest Wardens (protect wildlife), the Spirit Walkers (communicate with nature spirits), and the Emerald Rangers (patrol forests).

🌋 Pyrath

The Great Forge Council (controls industry), the Engineers Consortium (inventors, builders, scientists), the Mountain Rangers (protect mines), and the Royal Smithing Guild (the highest-ranked blacksmiths).

☁ Aetheria

The Celestial Assembly (kingdom government), the Academy of Arcana (largest magical academy), the Sky Navigators (chart wind routes), and the Astronomer's Circle (studies the stars).

🌊 Azure Reach

The Grand Fleet (protects the seas), the Explorer's Guild (discovers islands), the Cartographer Society (makes maps), and the Harbor Authority (controls ports).

🏜 Khar'Duun

The Imperial Archaeological Society (largest archaeology organization), the Desert Rangers (protect caravans), the Temple Guardians (protect tombs), and the Caravan Guild (trade).

☠ Blackfen

The Circle of Reeds (protect wetlands), the Great Hunters (monster specialists), the Marsh Guides (escort travelers), and the River Wardens (protect waterways).

🔮 Mordrath

The Keepers (government), the Library of Silence (protect forbidden knowledge), the Shadow Watch (investigate strange events), and the Dream Scholars (research dreams).

Universal Guilds

These exist in every kingdom.

  • ⚔ Adventurer's GuildThe most famous guild, accepts anyone. Ranks F→E→D→C→B→A→S→SS→SSS. Members get jobs, rewards, housing, and reputation.
  • 🏹 Hunter's GuildMonster hunting, tracking, creature research
  • 🔨 Craftsman's GuildBlacksmiths, builders, tailors
  • 🧪 Alchemist GuildPotions, poisons, medicine
  • 📚 Scholar's GuildHistory, research, ancient ruins
  • 🚢 Explorer's GuildMaps, ruins, unknown lands
  • 🏥 Healer's GuildHospitals, medicine, emergency aid
  • 🎭 Artist GuildMusic, painting, stories, festivals
  • Merchant GuildBanks, trading, loans, markets, auctions

Secret Organizations

These shape the world's biggest mysteries — global, not tied to one kingdom.

  • ⭐ The Order of NineNobody knows if they exist — nine members, one per kingdom, protecting balance. Even kings don't know them all.
  • ⭐ The First KeepersThe oldest organization, possibly older than the kingdoms. Protects Ancient ruins, never interferes openly.
  • ⭐ Children of TwilightOperate inside Mordrath, protecting forgotten knowledge
  • ⭐ Emerald CircleProtects nature, works across kingdoms
  • ⭐ Ember CircleLegendary craftsmen, invite-only masters
  • ⭐ Astral CircleStudies space, moons, ancient sky technology
  • ⭐ The Deep CartographersMap places nobody should know exist
  • ⭐ Keepers of HistoryCollect forgotten books — some believe they know why the Ancients disappeared

The Great Secret

All organizations, even the oldest ones, unknowingly reference a forgotten symbol in their oldest records: a circle divided into nine sections, with a tenth empty space at its center. No surviving document explains its meaning. The Order of Nine refuses to discuss it. The Keepers of History have removed several books that mention it. Some scholars believe the symbol predates the Ancients themselves.
