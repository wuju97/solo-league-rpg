How a party fight actually works
  1. Form the party (invite members above)
  2. Each member goes to World Map, finds the same target — a political hierarchy NPC, a Champion, or a Primordial — and clicks Challenge, picking their own loadout. This automatically tags their pick to this party.
  3. Each member also clicks Ready Up here — a separate step from challenging.
  4. Once everyone who's ready has a matching challenge locked in, the leader's Start Party Battle button works.

Inviting someone brings them into your world — their location moves to match yours, but their own class, rank, cards, and equipment stay entirely their own. Party members can pick from each other's cards when building a loadout. Creature fights can't be done as a party — only political hierarchy, Champions, and Primordials.
