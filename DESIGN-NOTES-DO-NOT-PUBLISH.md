# AETHERIS — TRUE HISTORY (GM ANSWER KEY — NOT PUBLIC)

**This file is not linked anywhere on the website and is not visible to players.**
It exists purely as your own design reference for writing future hints, book excerpts,
NPC dialogue, and prophecy fragments. Nothing here should ever appear verbatim in a
player-facing tab — only fragments, rumors, and partial (sometimes contradictory) truths,
the way each kingdom's "false history" already does.

---

## Before The Ancients
The Ancients were not the first civilization — they were the first to *write* history.
Before them, something older existed. Not kingdoms, not gods, not monsters. Something older.

## The Nine Primordials
Nine Primordials exist, each representing a law of existence. Not gods — they didn't
create the world, they simply awoke with it. None are good or evil; they maintain existence.

1. **The First Root** — Life (Eldergrove)
2. **The Endless Tide** — Change (Azure Reach)
3. **The World Forge** — Creation (Pyrath)
4. **The Veiled Shadow** — Memory (Mordrath)
5. **The Frost Father** — Time (Frosthaven)
6. **The Golden Sun** — Hope (Khar'Duun)
7. **The Sky Loom** — Knowledge (Aetheria)
8. **The Mire Heart** — Survival (Blackfen)
9. **The Crown Stag** — Balance (Valoria)

## The Tenth — The Empty One
Never named, drawn, or described. Every record mentioning it after the fact has been erased.

## The Ancients' True Nature
Researchers, builders, explorers, scientists, philosophers, engineers — not kings. They
didn't conquer, they learned. Their goal was understanding reality itself.

## The Great Discovery — The Foundation
They discovered something beneath reality itself, called only "The Foundation." Everything
rests upon it.

## The Nine Keys
Never weapons or artifacts — they were **permissions**, each granting access to one layer
of reality. No single person ever held all nine.

## The Ninth Gate
Not a gate — a **threshold**. A boundary between the world and the Foundation.

## Why It Was Sealed
Reality is not permanent — it requires maintenance. The Primordials unconsciously maintain
it; the Foundation keeps existence stable; the Ninth Gate protects access.

## The Greatest Mistake
Some Ancients believed reality could be improved. They divided — not into armies, into
philosophies. Someone eventually attempted to open the Ninth Gate.

## The Silence
No explosion, no war. Reality paused for one moment. Only the Ancients noticed. They
understood: if the Gate fully opened, existence itself could unravel.

## The Sacrifice
Not everyone agreed, but enough did. Instead of closing the Gate, the Ancients became its
seal — willingly surrendering physical existence, binding themselves into the Gate so it
could never fully open. They did not die. They became part of the lock.

## Why Cities Remain Untouched
They left peacefully — homes, meals, libraries all remained. No destruction, no panic.
Just absence.

## The False Histories (each kingdom is partly right)
- **Valoria** — believes they vanished
- **Khar'Duun** — believes they ascended
- **Aetheria** — believes they traveled beyond the stars
- **Mordrath** — believes they still exist
- **Eldergrove** — believes they became nature
- **Pyrath** — believes they became fire
- **Azure Reach** — believes they crossed the sea
- **Frosthaven** — believes they sleep beneath the ice
- **Blackfen** — believes they never left

## The Living Seal
The nine Primordials continue reinforcing the Gate without realizing it. If one Primordial
weakens, reality weakens.

## Why World Bosses Awaken
Not evil — they're responding. The Seal is weakening, and bosses try to restore balance
the only way they understand.

## The Three Moons (built by the Ancients, not natural)
- **Lumis** stabilizes Matter
- **Nyra** stabilizes Memory
- **Veyl** stabilizes Magic

If one moon fails, reality changes.

## Mordrath's Origin
The Veiled Shadow stirred long ago; its influence slightly altered reality, creating
permanent twilight. Not darkness, not corruption — just a shift.

## Unknown Species #000
Not a creature — a fragment of the Empty One. Not alive by normal definition.

## The Tenth Throne
Not abandoned — an observation station. Someone or something still watches Aetheris.
(Intentionally left unresolved: surviving Ancient? different civilization? something else?
— reserved for future expansions.)

## The Final Prophecy
Hidden separately in nine ancient locations, one verse per kingdom. Only combined do they read:

> When the Nine stand as One,
> The Gate shall remember its Keepers.
> When Balance falls,
> The Silent will speak.
> When the Forgotten are remembered,
> The Empty shall knock.
> Not to conquer.
> Not to destroy.
> But to be welcomed.
> Choose wisely —
> For the world shall answer only once.

No one in the current age possesses all nine verses.

## The Endgame Philosophy
The Ninth Gate should NOT be a final boss — that would end the mystery. Instead, opening it
(if players ever collectively achieve that) should transform the world rather than conclude
it: a new continent, restored civilizations, awakened regions, changed magic/exploration
rules. This gives the game room to evolve for years instead of ending on one fight.

### Design note
Every major mystery already placed in World Lore (the per-kingdom "Great Mystery" entries,
the Nine Great Mysteries list in Timeline, the Primordial Creatures in Creatures) is a
deliberately partial fragment of the truth above. Keep it that way — new content (books,
NPC dialogue, ruin inscriptions, prophecy verses) should keep dropping fragments and
contradictions, never the full picture.
