Kingdom Specialties

Each kingdom's signature market sells exclusive items found nowhere else.

Intended rule: buying from a kingdom's specialty shop should require actually being in that kingdom — either directly from the NPC running it, or as a reward from a quest they give. Not yet enforced, since player location tracking doesn't exist.

🏰 Valoria — Royal Equipment

Signature market: Lion's Square

Known for craftsmanship. Exclusive: Knight Armor, Royal Shields, Banners, Heavy Plate, Lances, Honor Medals.

Best for: Knight, Paladin, Templar

❄ Frosthaven — Survival Gear

Signature market: Icewind Bazaar

Exclusive: Fur Cloaks, Ice Weapons, Warm Potions, Snow Boots, Hunting Spears, Frost Charms.

Best for: Hunter, Ranger

🌳 Eldergrove — Nature

Signature market: Bloom Market

Exclusive: Herbs, Seeds, Nature Staves, Spirit Charms, Animal Treats, Forest Cloaks.

Best for: Druid, Beastmaster

🌋 Pyrath — Forging

Signature market: Ember Forge District

Exclusive: High-quality Weapons, Ores, Gems, Upgrade Stones, Tool Kits, Heavy Hammers.

Best for: Berserker, Engineer

☁ Aetheria — Magic

Signature market: Sky Arcane Exchange

Exclusive: Spell Books, Magic Crystals, Wands, Enchantments, Mana Potions, Scrolls.

Best for: Mage, Bard

🌊 Azure Reach — Trading

Signature market: Blue Harbor Market

Exclusive: Fishing Gear, Maps, Ships, Navigation Tools, Rare Fish, Sea Charms, plus exotic imports.

Best for: Explorer, Ranger

🏜 Khar'Duun — Ancient Relics

Signature market: Golden Dunes Bazaar

Exclusive: Ancient Coins, Relics, Sand Charms, Treasure Maps, Archaeology Tools, Rare Jewelry.

Best for: Treasure Hunters

☠ Blackfen — Alchemy

Signature market: Mist Hollow Market

Exclusive: Rare Mushrooms, Venoms, Poison Kits, Medicine, Swamp Herbs, Toxins.

Best for: Alchemist

🔮 Mordrath — Forbidden Items

Signature market: Nightveil Exchange

Exclusive: Shadow Robes, Cursed Relics, Dark Crystals, Masks, Forgotten Books, Nightmare Charms.

Best for: Necromancer
